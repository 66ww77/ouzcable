Technical Data

Technical Specifications

Representative 26/35 kV XLPE cable construction per IEC 60502-2. Final design is tailored to your project requirements.

Rated voltage U0/U (Um)26/35 (40.5) kV
ConductorCopper or aluminium, class 2 stranded & compacted
Conductor screenSemi-conductive compound (extruded)
InsulationXLPE — cross-linked polyethylene, 90 °C continuous
Insulation screenSemi-conductive layer + copper wire & tape metallic screen
Bedding / separationExtruded or wrapped separator
ArmourGalvanized steel wire (SWA) or aluminium wire
Outer sheathPVC (ST2) or LSZH, flame retardant
Short-circuit rating250 °C max. conductor temperature, 5 s
StandardsIEC 60502-2 · GB/T 12706.2 · GOST 31996
Min. bending radius15 × D (single core) / 12 × D (multi-core)
InstallationDirect burial, ducts, cable trays, air, indoor & outdoor

* All cables are manufactured and tested in our own factory. Final electrical and mechanical parameters are confirmed in the datasheet for your specific cable configuration.

About Us

OUZHI Cable — Source Factory of 35 kV Power Cables

OUZHI Cable is a source factory and manufacturer of medium- and high-voltage power cables — not a trading company.
