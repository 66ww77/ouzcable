OUZHI Cable (欧智电缆) is a genuine source factory for 26/35 kV XLPE medium-voltage power cables — not a trading company. We design, produce, test and export IEC-certified 35 kV cable systems directly from our own production lines to customers across Central Asia, the Middle East, Russia, Europe and the Americas.

Who We Are

Founded as a manufacturing enterprise, OUZHI Cable owns the complete production chain for medium-voltage power cables: copper and aluminium conductors, XLPE insulation, copper wire and tape screens, SWA or aluminium armour, and PVC or LSZH sheaths. Because we are the factory, we control quality, pricing and delivery from the first drawing to the port — with no middlemen and no trading margins.

Our 35 kV cable line is produced end-to-end on our own modern extrusion, screening and armouring equipment, followed by rigorous in-house high-voltage testing. Every reel that leaves our factory complies with IEC 60502-2, GB/T 12706.2 and GOST 31996, backed by ISO 9001 quality management.
