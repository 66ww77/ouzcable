Why Most 35 kV Cable Purchases Go Wrong — and How to Avoid It

International buyers of medium-voltage cables often encounter three costly problems:

01
Trading Company Markups
Middlemen add 15-30% on top of factory prices while providing zero technical value. The cable arrives with no traceable manufacturing records.
02
Substandard Materials
Some suppliers substitute copper with copper-clad aluminium, or use recycled XLPE that fails partial discharge testing — causing cable failure within months.
03
Missing Certification
Cables shipped without proper test reports or conformity certificates get held at customs or rejected by project inspectors on site.

OUZHI Cable eliminates all three risks: we are the factory, we use only virgin materials, and every reel ships with full IEC 60502-2 / GOST 31996 test documentation.
