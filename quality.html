FAQ — Quality & Certification

Can you provide type test reports?
Yes — IEC/GOST/GB type test reports from our accredited laboratory are available on request.
Do you allow third-party inspection?
Absolutely — SGS, TÜV, BV or your nominated inspector is welcome during production and before shipment.
What is the warranty period?
We provide a 12-month warranty against manufacturing defects; extended terms can be agreed per contract.
Do you offer certificates for EAC markets?
Yes — EAC declaration/certification documentation for Russia, CIS and Central Asia is prepared with every shipment.
How do I verify quality before ordering?
Request samples, test reports and a factory audit — we welcome serious buyers at our plant.
